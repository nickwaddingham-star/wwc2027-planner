## Rules when changing photos

- Use only photos whose licence allows reuse, and keep the photographer, licence and source link beside each one.
- CC BY-SA photos that are edited must be shared under the same licence.

## To check before an official release

- The whale photo was taken at Abrolhos, not Praia do Forte.
- The tile photo shows artwork by Athos Bulcão, which may have its own copyright, separate from the photo's licence.
